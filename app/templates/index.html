Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers regarding quizzes, anti-cheating, and certificates.

QuizVerse automatically monitors window focus and tab switches during timed assessments. Students receive visual warnings upon leaving the exam tab, and on the 3rd violation, the engine automatically locks and submits the attempt.

When a student achieves the required passing score (e.g. 70%), QuizVerse utilizes ReportLab on the server to dynamically generate a personalized landscape PDF certificate complete with student name, quiz title, score, and issue date.

In the Admin/Examiner Dashboard, teachers can upload standard `.csv` files containing columns for Question Text, Option A, Option B, Option C, Option D, and Correct Answer. The platform auto-creates the questions instantly.
